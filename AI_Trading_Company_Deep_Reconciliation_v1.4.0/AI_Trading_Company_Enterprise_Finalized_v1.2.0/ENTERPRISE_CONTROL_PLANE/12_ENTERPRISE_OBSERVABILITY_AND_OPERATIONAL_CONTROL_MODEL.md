# Enterprise Observability and Operational Control Model

## Status
Controlled operations standard.

## Required Signals
- service health
- command/event flow
- latency
- failures
- queue/backlog state
- data quality
- provider health
- reconciliation state
- risk-control decisions
- security events
- release state

## Evidence
Operational telemetry must be correlated with the applicable command, event, deployment, or incident when technically possible.

Observability does not replace durable business/audit records.
