# Enterprise Identity, Access, and Privileged Operation Model

## Status
Controlled security standard.

## Principles
- deny by default
- least privilege
- separation of duties
- explicit service identity
- short-lived credentials where supported
- privileged actions require stronger controls
- access review and revocation are lifecycle processes

## Financial Authority
AI agents, research components, UI clients, and notification channels do not acquire financial authority merely by possessing credentials to another system. Financial authority is determined by the canonical permission and execution controls.
