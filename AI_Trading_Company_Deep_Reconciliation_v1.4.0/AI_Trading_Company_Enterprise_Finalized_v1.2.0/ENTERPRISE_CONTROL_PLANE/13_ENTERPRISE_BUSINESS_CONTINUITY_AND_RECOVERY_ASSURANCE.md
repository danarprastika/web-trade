# Enterprise Business Continuity and Recovery Assurance

## Status
Controlled planning standard.

## Recovery Domains
- application services
- persistence
- market data
- broker/provider connectivity
- identity/secrets dependencies
- audit evidence
- notification/control channels

## Recovery Principle
Recovery procedures must preserve financial-state integrity. A service restart must not be treated as reconciliation.

Recovery targets, thresholds, and account/provider-specific values remain subject to approved decisions.
