# Enterprise Risk Acceptance and Exception Standard

## Status
Controlled governance standard.

## Principle
Exceptions are temporary, explicit, scoped, owned, and reviewable. An exception cannot weaken a non-waivable safety or authority rule.

## Required Exception Record
- exception ID
- affected requirement/control
- reason
- risk statement
- scope
- compensating controls
- owner
- approver
- start and expiry
- review date
- evidence
- closure decision

## Fail-Closed Rule
Missing approval, expired exception, or ambiguous scope means the affected activity remains blocked.
