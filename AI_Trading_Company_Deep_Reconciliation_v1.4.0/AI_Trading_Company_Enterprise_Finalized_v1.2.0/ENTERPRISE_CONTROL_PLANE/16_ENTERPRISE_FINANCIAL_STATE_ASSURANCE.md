# Enterprise Financial State Assurance

## Status
Controlled financial-control intent. Not accounting or legal advice.

## Scope
- cash and balance representations
- positions
- fills
- fees
- realized/unrealized P&L
- transfers
- reconciliation
- financial reporting evidence

## Principle
Derived portfolio values must be traceable to authoritative transactions/fills and approved valuation logic. Discrepancies must be visible and must not be silently normalized.
