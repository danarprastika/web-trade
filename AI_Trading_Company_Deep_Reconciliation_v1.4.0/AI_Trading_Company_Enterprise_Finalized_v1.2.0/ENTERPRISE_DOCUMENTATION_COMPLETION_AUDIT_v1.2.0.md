# Enterprise Documentation Completion Audit v1.2.0

## Scope
This audit evaluates the v1.1.0 controlled documentation baseline as a source-preserving enterprise documentation package. Existing source documents are retained; additions are layered on top rather than silently rewriting unresolved technical or governance decisions.

## Improvements added
1. Enterprise operating model and separation of duties.
2. Enterprise risk register/control framework.
3. Jurisdiction-aware compliance/legal control framework.
4. Financial control and accounting assurance.
5. Vendor and third-party governance.
6. Enterprise data stewardship.
7. Strategy lifecycle/promotion governance.
8. Model risk and AI assurance.
9. Service catalog, SRE and error-budget governance.
10. Identity lifecycle/access review.
11. Audit evidence and records management.
12. Release/change approval and architecture review.
13. Business continuity and crisis management.
14. Capacity/cost/resource governance.
15. Market/account onboarding governance.
16. Incident communication governance.
17. Enterprise document control.

## Explicit non-claims
- No legal or regulatory conclusion is asserted.
- No risk limit, capital limit, performance target, SLO value, retention period, staffing plan or deployment size is invented.
- No unresolved Phase 0 decision is silently approved.
- No implementation or live-trading authorization is granted.

## Remaining controlled work
The package is substantially more enterprise-complete, but “documentation complete” does not mean implementation-ready for every capability. Before G1 implementation authorization, the applicable change requests, cross-document contract validation, unresolved source conflicts, exact schema mappings, validator behavior and entry evidence must be explicitly closed under the project governance process.
