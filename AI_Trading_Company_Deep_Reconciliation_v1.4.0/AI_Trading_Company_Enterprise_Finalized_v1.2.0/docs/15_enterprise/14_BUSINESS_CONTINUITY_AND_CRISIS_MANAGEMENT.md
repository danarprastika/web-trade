# Business Continuity and Crisis Management

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline; subordinate to the applicable Normative Core and approved contracts.

## Purpose
Coordinate technical recovery and organizational response to severe disruptions.

## Authority and interpretation
- This document defines control intent and operating expectations; it does not silently approve unresolved values, credentials, legal positions, capital limits, or live activation.
- Where a value is marked TBD, it requires explicit owner-approved decision evidence before implementation may depend on it.
- Same-tier conflicts are fail-closed and require a recorded change request or decision.

## Scenario classes
Broker outage, market-data outage, cloud/region outage, credential compromise, database corruption, model/provider outage, security incident, severe reconciliation divergence and operational staffing loss.

## Crisis roles
Incident commander, technical lead, trading/risk lead, security lead, communications lead and evidence custodian as applicable.

## Continuity priorities
Protect capital and safety controls first; preserve authoritative state and evidence; restore reconciliation; restore controlled operations; resume normal change flow.

## Exercises
Game days validate runbooks, dependencies, contact paths, recovery evidence and decision rights. Findings become tracked corrective actions.
