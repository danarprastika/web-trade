# Compliance and Legal Control Framework

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline; subordinate to the applicable Normative Core and approved contracts.

## Purpose
Create an enterprise placeholder for jurisdiction-specific obligations without asserting legal conclusions.

## Authority and interpretation
- This document defines control intent and operating expectations; it does not silently approve unresolved values, credentials, legal positions, capital limits, or live activation.
- Where a value is marked TBD, it requires explicit owner-approved decision evidence before implementation may depend on it.
- Same-tier conflicts are fail-closed and require a recorded change request or decision.

## Scope
Entity obligations, trading permissions, market access, broker/exchange terms, recordkeeping, privacy, sanctions screening where applicable, tax/accounting obligations, employment/vendor obligations and regulatory reporting where applicable.

## Jurisdiction register
Maintain a versioned register of jurisdictions, legal entities, markets, counterparties, account types, applicable obligations, responsible owner and evidence source.

## Pre-launch control
No market or broker is enabled for live activity until the applicable legal/compliance review is recorded as satisfied or formally waived by the authorized owner under the governance process.

## Records
Preserve source documents, interpretations, approvals, review dates and superseded versions. Do not rely on undocumented assumptions.

## External counsel
Where legal interpretation is required, obtain qualified professional review. This document is an engineering control framework, not legal advice.
