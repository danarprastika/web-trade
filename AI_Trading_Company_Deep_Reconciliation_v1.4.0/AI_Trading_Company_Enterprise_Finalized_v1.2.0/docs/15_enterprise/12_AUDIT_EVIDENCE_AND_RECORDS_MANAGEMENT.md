# Audit Evidence and Records Management

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline; subordinate to the applicable Normative Core and approved contracts.

## Purpose
Define a durable evidence system that supports reconstruction of important decisions and system behavior.

## Authority and interpretation
- This document defines control intent and operating expectations; it does not silently approve unresolved values, credentials, legal positions, capital limits, or live activation.
- Where a value is marked TBD, it requires explicit owner-approved decision evidence before implementation may depend on it.
- Same-tier conflicts are fail-closed and require a recorded change request or decision.

## Evidence classes
Requirement; decision; configuration; deployment; test; model/prompt evaluation; approval; incident; reconciliation; provider certification; access review; operational review.

## Integrity
Evidence is append-only or versioned, timestamped, attributable, linked by correlation identifiers where applicable, and protected from unauthorized alteration.

## Retention
Retention periods are governed by approved policy and applicable obligations. Never invent a universal period here.

## Retrieval
Evidence must be discoverable by system, environment, market, service, version, decision/change ID and time range where applicable.

## Destruction
Deletion follows approved lifecycle policy and legal hold requirements where applicable; destruction itself is evidenced.
