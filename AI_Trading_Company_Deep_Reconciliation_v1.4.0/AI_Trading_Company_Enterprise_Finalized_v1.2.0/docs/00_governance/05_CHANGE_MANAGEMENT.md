# Change Management

> Status: FINAL FROZEN BASELINE v1.0 — subordinate to FINAL_SPECIFICATION/.

## Purpose
Control changes to code, models, prompts, strategies, policies, data schemas and infrastructure.

## Change classes

Normal, urgent, emergency and breaking.

## Production rule

Live-impacting changes require impact analysis, tests, approval, deployment evidence and rollback plan.

## Emergency

Emergency changes may reduce functionality or stop trading, but may not erase audit evidence or bypass safety gates.
