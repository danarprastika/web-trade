# Requirements Traceability

> Status: FINAL FROZEN BASELINE v1.0 — subordinate to FINAL_SPECIFICATION/.

## Purpose
Connect business requirements to architecture, implementation and tests.

## Trace chain

Requirement → domain contract → interface/state/event → implementation → test → evidence.

## Critical requirements

Risk authority, exact order approval, reconciliation gate, credential isolation, environment isolation, immutable OOS, kill switch and audit integrity must each have direct test evidence.
