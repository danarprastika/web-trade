# Project Constitution

> Status: FINAL FROZEN BASELINE v1.0 — subordinate to FINAL_SPECIFICATION/.

## Purpose
Define the immutable identity and laws of the system.

## Identity

AI-native proprietary trading organization. AI/agent swarm is an intelligence and operations layer supporting proprietary trading, not the product identity itself.

## Mission

Research, validate, govern and operate systematic trading across Crypto, Forex, Stocks and Commodities while protecting capital and operational integrity.

## Authority

Owner retains emergency control. One authoritative Risk Engine owns final live risk decisions. OMS owns order intent. Execution owns broker interaction. No AI agent receives unrestricted live-order authority.

## Non-negotiables

Least privilege; exact-intent risk approval; reconciliation gate; immutable OOS artifacts; isolated live credentials; append-only decision evidence; governed Telegram/Web commands; fail-closed critical uncertainty; versioned live behavior.

## V1 boundary

Personal-use proprietary trading. No customers, SaaS, billing, social trading, public strategy marketplace or multi-tenancy.

## Future boundary

Architecture may evolve into a formal proprietary trading company after appropriate legal, compliance, treasury and operational structures exist.
