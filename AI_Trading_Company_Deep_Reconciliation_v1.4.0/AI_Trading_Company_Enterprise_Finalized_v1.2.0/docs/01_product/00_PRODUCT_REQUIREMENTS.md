# Product Requirements Document

> Status: FINAL FROZEN BASELINE v1.0 — subordinate to FINAL_SPECIFICATION/.

## Purpose
Describe the product as an AI-native proprietary trading operating system.

## Primary users

Single owner/operator in V1.

## Capabilities

AI company orchestration, market intelligence, research, strategy lifecycle, risk, trading, execution, portfolio, performance, audit and operations.

## Market scope

Crypto, Forex, Stocks and Commodities with independent modules.

## Interfaces

Web/App primary; Telegram secondary.

## Success criteria

Safe automation, reproducible research, transparent decisions, operational resilience and controlled progression from research to demo and potentially live.
