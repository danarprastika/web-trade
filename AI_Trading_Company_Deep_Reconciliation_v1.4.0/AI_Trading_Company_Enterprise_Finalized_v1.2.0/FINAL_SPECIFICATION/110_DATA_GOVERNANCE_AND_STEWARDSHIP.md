# 110 Data Governance And Stewardship

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline.

## Purpose
Enterprise data ownership, classification, quality, lineage and lifecycle controls.

## Normative boundary
This contract supplements the existing FINAL_SPECIFICATION set. It does not override the Normative Core, Risk authority, security controls, human approval requirements, production gates, or explicit owner decisions.

## Required implementation behavior
- Version the applicable policy and contract artifacts.
- Record accountable ownership and approval.
- Preserve audit evidence and reproducibility.
- Fail closed when a critical control is ambiguous or conflicting.
- Do not invent numerical limits, legal conclusions, credentials, provider guarantees, or live activation authority.

## Evidence
Implementation must link code, configuration, tests, operational evidence and approvals to the applicable requirement/change/decision identifiers.
