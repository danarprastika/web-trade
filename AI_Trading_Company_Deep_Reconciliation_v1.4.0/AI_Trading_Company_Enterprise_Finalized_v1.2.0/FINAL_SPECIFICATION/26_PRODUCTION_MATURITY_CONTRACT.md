# PRODUCTION MATURITY CONTRACT
## Beyond MVP — Production-Grade Completion

Status: NORMATIVE IMPLEMENTATION CONTROL

## Objective

This project MUST NOT be treated as an MVP-only application. Implementation must progress toward a production-grade proprietary trading platform.

## Maturity levels

### M0 — Foundation
Domain primitives, contracts, persistence foundations, test infrastructure.

### M1 — Functional Trading System
Market data, trading core, risk, OMS, reconciliation, research and required interfaces operate in controlled environments.

### M2 — Controlled Autonomous System
AI agents, research lifecycle, demo/paper, shadow operation, monitoring, failure handling and governance operate under explicit permissions.

### M3 — Production Candidate
Security, resilience, observability, performance, backup/restore, deployment, provider certification, incident response and operational controls satisfy production criteria.

### M4 — Live-Capable
All live prerequisites, approvals, operational readiness, reconciliation, kill switches, credentials, human authority and certification are satisfied.

## Rule

Passing an early gate does not mean the product is production-ready.

The implementation roadmap MUST preserve the distinction between functional completeness and production readiness.
