# Enterprise AI, Model, and Agent Assurance

## Status
Controlled AI governance standard.

## Scope
- model identity/version
- prompt/instruction version
- tool permissions
- evaluation
- safety constraints
- output validation
- provenance
- memory/data boundaries
- failure behavior
- rollback/deactivation

## Authority Boundary
AI systems may propose or analyze within their granted scope. They cannot self-grant permissions, bypass risk controls, or redefine production authority.
