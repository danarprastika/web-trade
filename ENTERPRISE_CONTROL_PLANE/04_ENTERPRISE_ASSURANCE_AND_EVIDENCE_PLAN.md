# Enterprise Assurance and Evidence Plan

## Status
Controlled planning document.

## Assurance Layers
1. Design assurance — architecture and control review.
2. Contract assurance — schemas, invariants, compatibility.
3. Implementation assurance — code and configuration evidence.
4. Test assurance — deterministic, integration, failure, security, and operational tests.
5. Operational assurance — monitoring, incident, recovery, reconciliation.
6. Governance assurance — approvals, exceptions, gate records.

## Evidence Standard
Evidence must identify:
- subject and scope
- exact version/build/configuration
- test or review procedure
- result
- timestamp
- actor
- retained artifact
- relationship to the requirement/control

Evidence must not be represented as approval unless the applicable approval authority recorded it.
