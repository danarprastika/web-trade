# Enterprise Control Plane Charter

## Status
Controlled documentation baseline. This document defines governance structure and control intent; it does not authorize implementation or live trading.

## Purpose
Provide the enterprise-level control plane that connects requirements, architecture, security, risk, data, AI/model governance, software delivery, operations, evidence, and release decisions.

## Objectives
1. Establish one navigable control system across the documentation corpus.
2. Make ownership, evidence, approval, and exception handling explicit.
3. Prevent undocumented architectural or operational drift.
4. Preserve fail-closed behavior where authority, safety, or production readiness is ambiguous.

## Non-Goals
- This document does not select unresolved technology options.
- It does not establish capital, risk, regulatory, or broker-specific limits.
- It does not grant implementation or live authority.

## Control Domains
- Governance and decision authority
- Product and requirements
- Architecture and engineering
- Trading, portfolio, and risk
- AI/model assurance
- Data and information management
- Security and identity
- Software delivery and change
- Reliability and operations
- Business continuity
- Financial/accounting assurance
- Vendor and market-access governance
- Audit evidence and records

## Decision Rule
When a control-domain document conflicts with a higher-authority normative document, the higher-authority rule prevails. Same-level unresolved conflicts stop affected work and require a controlled Change Request.
