# Enterprise Vendor and Provider Certification Standard

## Status
Controlled third-party governance standard.

## Certification Areas
- identity and access
- schema and API compatibility
- market data quality
- order/execution semantics
- rate limits and quotas
- failure behavior
- time/clock semantics
- operational support
- security posture
- continuity/recovery
- commercial and legal review

A provider is not production-approved solely because an adapter compiles or passes a happy-path test.
