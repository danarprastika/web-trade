# Product Non-Functional Requirements

> Status: FINAL FROZEN BASELINE v1.0 — subordinate to FINAL_SPECIFICATION/.

## Purpose
Define measurable quality expectations.

## Safety

Fail closed on critical unknowns.

## Reliability

Recoverable state and idempotent external mutations.

## Observability

Every critical flow is traceable.

## Performance

Explicit latency budgets rather than vague speed requirements.

## Security

Least privilege and credential isolation.

## Maintainability

Contracts and domain logic remain independent of providers and UI.
