# Enterprise Risk Register and Control Framework

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline; subordinate to the applicable Normative Core and approved contracts.

## Purpose
Provide a company-level taxonomy for risks spanning trading, technology, data, AI, security, operations, vendors and governance.

## Authority and interpretation
- This document defines control intent and operating expectations; it does not silently approve unresolved values, credentials, legal positions, capital limits, or live activation.
- Where a value is marked TBD, it requires explicit owner-approved decision evidence before implementation may depend on it.
- Same-tier conflicts are fail-closed and require a recorded change request or decision.

## Risk domains
Market/trading; liquidity; counterparty; model; data; AI/agent; cyber; infrastructure; availability; operational; vendor; compliance/legal; financial reporting; change/release; concentration; reputational/communications.

## Risk record
Risk ID, statement, owner, affected capability, causes, consequences, existing controls, residual exposure, indicators, treatment, target date, evidence and review cadence.

## Control types
Preventive, detective, corrective, compensating and recovery controls. Critical controls require explicit test evidence and an owner.

## Escalation
Breaches or material uncertainty move to the appropriate control owner. Unknown severity or conflicting evidence is fail-closed until assessed.

## No invented thresholds
Numerical risk appetite, exposure limits, drawdown limits and alert thresholds must come from approved risk-policy artifacts, not this framework.
