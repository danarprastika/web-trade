# Enterprise Document Control Standard

> Status: ENTERPRISE DOCUMENTATION BASELINE v1.2.0 — controlled planning baseline; subordinate to the applicable Normative Core and approved contracts.

## Purpose
Make the documentation set itself behave like a controlled enterprise system of records.

## Authority and interpretation
- This document defines control intent and operating expectations; it does not silently approve unresolved values, credentials, legal positions, capital limits, or live activation.
- Where a value is marked TBD, it requires explicit owner-approved decision evidence before implementation may depend on it.
- Same-tier conflicts are fail-closed and require a recorded change request or decision.

## Document metadata
Document ID, title, version, status, authority tier, owner, approver, effective date, review date, dependencies and supersedes/superseded-by links.

## Statuses
Draft, review, approved, effective, deprecated, superseded, retired. “Final” means final for the stated governance scope, not proof that implementation is complete.

## Change traceability
Every normative change has a change ID and reason. Editorial corrections do not change authority or policy meaning.

## Integrity
Index, manifests, checksums and cross-references are regenerated after controlled updates.

## Review cadence
Critical contracts are reviewed after material architecture, security, provider or trading-control changes and at an approved periodic cadence.
