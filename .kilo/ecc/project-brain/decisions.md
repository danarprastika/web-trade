| EXD-011 | WI-117's AC4 durable-buffering gap is closed by the transactional outbox (WI-120 + WI-121), not by a write-ahead log and not inside WI-117 alone. `Guard.pending` becomes `count(*) FROM audit_outbox WHERE dispatched_at IS NULL`, and the audit record plus its signed checkpoint are committed in the same transaction as the business mutation | `docs/22` §4 requires the audit stream to be written "transactionally with the business mutation or outbox event", and `docs/08` §13 refuses to trade a safety invariant against availability. Wiring the write path first would make the first crash the first loss. The crash path is already detected — `verifyAgainstChain` refuses to boot with "registry has drifted from the evidence" — so today registry/audit corroboration is a load-bearing backstop, and it must be preserved or replaced by the new design rather than assumed | Accepted — sequencing constraint | `main.go:435`, `journal.go:368`/`:377`, `verifyAgainstChain`, WI-117 |
